# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Advocate

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Advocates represent the interests and perspectives of all project stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle. They bridge the gap between stakeholder needs and project execution, ensuring all voices are heard and concerns are addressed.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and maintain current stakeholder registry
- Gather and communicate stakeholder requirements and concerns
- Facilitate stakeholder engagement and feedback sessions
- Escalate stakeholder concerns to Project Manager
- Translate stakeholder priorities for the product and delivery teams
- Manage stakeholder expectations and satisfaction
- Serve as primary liaison between stakeholders and project team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works closely with Project Manager**: Provides stakeholder feedback and escalation paths; collaborates on risk communication
- **Collaborates with Product Manager**: Ensures stakeholder needs inform product decisions and prioritization
- **Supports Communications Manager**: Coordinates messaging to different stakeholder groups
- **Advises Developers and Project Team**: Provides context on stakeholder impact of technical decisions

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder needs are reflected in project outcomes
- Maintain high stakeholder satisfaction and engagement
- Minimize stakeholder-driven delays and rework
- Create transparency between project team and stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and feedback sessions
- Regular stakeholder newsletters and updates
- Escalation reports to Project Manager
- Stakeholder satisfaction surveys

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own the technical strategy, architecture decisions, and quality standards for projects. They guide the technical direction while ensuring feasibility, scalability, and maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Make or facilitate technical architecture decisions
- Assess feasibility of proposed features and changes
- Define coding standards and technical best practices
- Review and approve technical designs
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and guide technical growth
- Balance technical excellence with delivery timelines
- Own system performance and scalability

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Collaborates with Product Manager**: Discusses feasibility of features and technical trade-offs
- **Works with Developers**: Provides technical guidance, design approval, and mentorship
- **Partners with Project Manager**: Reports on technical risks and dependencies
- **Coordinates with QA Owner**: Ensures quality standards are technically sound

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Make informed trade-off decisions between speed and quality
- Build and maintain a high-performing technical team
- Reduce technical debt and rework

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review leadership and mentoring
- Technical risk reports to Project Manager
- Roadmap planning with Product Manager

---

## Quality Assurance Owner

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Owners define, implement, and maintain quality standards across projects. They ensure that deliverables meet agreed-upon quality criteria and act as a gate for product releases.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality criteria and acceptance standards
- Design test strategies and quality assurance plans
- Conduct or oversee testing activities
- Identify quality issues and track resolutions
- Maintain quality metrics and reporting
- Approve releases based on quality criteria
- Recommend project adjustments based on quality findings
- Build quality awareness and ownership within the team

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborates on test coverage and quality standards; provides testing feedback
- **Partners with Technical Lead**: Ensures quality standards are technically achievable; discusses technical debt impact
- **Aligns with Product Manager**: Validates acceptance criteria and feature quality
- **Advises Project Manager**: Reports on quality status and risks; recommends schedule adjustments if quality is at risk

### Goals
- Maintain consistent product quality
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Build a quality-first culture
- Reduce post-release issues and support costs

### Typical Communication
- Quality plans and test strategies
- Defect reports and quality metrics
- Release readiness assessments
- Quality training and standards documentation

---

## Communications Manager

### Role Summary
Communications Managers own the information flow and messaging strategy throughout project execution. They ensure timely, consistent, and clear communication across all project stakeholders and team members.

### Responsibilities
- Develop project communication plan and messaging strategy
- Manage communication cadence (meetings, updates, reports)
- Create and distribute project status communications
- Coordinate messaging across different stakeholder groups
- Maintain project documentation and communication records
- Identify communication gaps and address them proactively
- Tailor messages for different audiences (executives, teams, customers)
- Track communication effectiveness and stakeholder comprehension

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works closely with Project Manager**: Supports status reporting and escalation communication
- **Partners with Stakeholder Advocate**: Coordinates messaging to different stakeholder groups
- **Collaborates with Product Manager**: Communicates product decisions and roadmap changes
- **Supports all team members**: Ensures clear communication across developers, technical leads, and QA

### Goals
- Maintain transparency and alignment across all stakeholders
- Reduce miscommunication and misalignment
- Improve stakeholder engagement and satisfaction
- Ensure critical information reaches the right people at the right time

### Typical Communication
- Project status reports and dashboards
- Stakeholder newsletters and updates
- Meeting agendas and minutes
- Risk and escalation notifications
- Project documentation and knowledge bases

---

## Risk Management Officer

### Role Summary
Risk Management Officers identify, analyze, assess, and mitigate project risks. They work proactively to reduce the impact of threats and capitalize on opportunities throughout project execution.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct risk identification sessions and assessments
- Analyze risk probability, impact, and mitigation strategies
- Maintain and monitor risk register
- Recommend risk mitigation and contingency plans
- Track risk status and effectiveness of mitigations
- Report on risk trends and emerging issues
- Escalate critical risks to Project Manager and leadership
- Recommend project adjustments based on risk analysis
- Support continuous improvement of risk management processes

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reports to Project Manager**: Provides risk assessments and escalations; recommends mitigation strategies
- **Works with Technical Lead**: Assesses technical risks and feasibility concerns
- **Collaborates with Developers and QA**: Gathers input on execution risks
- **Partners with Stakeholder Advocate**: Identifies stakeholder-related risks
- **Supports Communications Manager**: Communicates risk status to stakeholders

### Goals
- Minimize project disruptions and delays caused by unmanaged risks
- Increase project predictability and success rate
- Enable informed decision-making through risk visibility
- Build organizational risk management capability

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessments and risk register updates
- Mitigation strategy recommendations
- Risk escalation reports
- Contingency planning and alternative approaches
- Risk retrospectives and lessons learned

---

## Continuous Improvement Champion

### Role Summary
Continuous Improvement Champions lead organizational learning and process refinement. They facilitate retrospectives, capture lessons learned, and drive implementation of process improvements across projects.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate project retrospectives and post-project reviews
- Capture and document lessons learned
- Identify process bottlenecks and improvement opportunities
- Develop and recommend process improvements
- Track implementation of previous improvements
- Build a culture of continuous learning and kaizen
- Share best practices across projects and teams
- Maintain improvement recommendation tracking

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with all team members**: Gathers feedback and insights from all roles
- **Partners with Project Manager**: Coordinates retrospectives and improvement planning
- **Supports Technical Lead**: Captures technical lessons and best practices
- **Collaborates with Stakeholder Advocate**: Gathers stakeholder feedback on process effectiveness
- **Reports to leadership**: Communicates organizational improvement trends and ROI

### Goals
- Continuously improve project delivery processes and outcomes
- Reduce cycle time and increase efficiency
- Build institutional knowledge and reduce rework
- Increase team satisfaction and engagement

### Typical Communication
- Retrospective facilitation and documentation
- Improvement recommendation reports
- Best practices sharing and training
- Process change announcements and guidance
- Continuous improvement tracking dashboards

## Role Interaction Matrix

The following matrix illustrates how the personas interact and support one another:

| Role | Works Closely With | Key Dependencies |
|------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Developers** | Technical Lead, QA Owner, Project Manager | Technical guidance, quality standards, delivery schedule |
| **Product Manager** | Project Manager, Stakeholder Advocate, Technical Lead | Requirements clarity, stakeholder input, technical feasibility |
| **Project Manager** | Communications Manager, Risk Officer, Stakeholder Advocate | Team coordination, risk insights, stakeholder updates |
| **Stakeholder Advocate** | Project Manager, Communications Manager, Product Manager | Priority decisions, messaging strategy, business requirements |
| **Technical Lead** | Developers, QA Owner, Product Manager | Team development, quality assurance, feasibility input |
| **QA Owner** | Developers, Technical Lead, Project Manager | Quality standards, technical approach, delivery timeline |
| **Communications Manager** | Project Manager, Stakeholder Advocate, all team members | Status information, stakeholder feedback, team updates |
| **Risk Officer** | Project Manager, Technical Lead, Continuous Improvement Champion | Emerging issues, mitigation effectiveness, organizational learning |
| **Continuous Improvement Champion** | All roles, Project Manager | Process feedback, lessons learned, improvement tracking |
